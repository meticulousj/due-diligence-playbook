# Design Brainstorm: The Due Diligence Playbook Landing Page

## Response 1: Modern Institutional (Probability: 0.08)

**Design Movement:** Contemporary B2B SaaS with institutional authority  
**Core Principles:**
- Precision and clarity through restrained typography
- Hierarchical information architecture that guides decision-making
- Subtle depth through layered cards and soft shadows
- Confidence through asymmetric, left-aligned layouts

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep navy (rgb(15, 23, 42)) as primary—conveys trust, stability, and professional rigor
- Gold accent (rgb(217, 119, 6))—signals premium value and expertise
- Charcoal text on light backgrounds, white on dark
- Minimal use of color; restraint is the statement

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric grid with left-aligned content blocks
- Hero section: split layout with text on left, abstract geometric pattern on right
- Domain cards arranged in a staggered 2-3 column grid
- Sticky sidebar navigation for deal professionals

**Signature Elements:**
- Vertical gold line accent on left edge of key sections
- Minimalist checkmark icons for each domain
- Subtle grid background pattern in hero section
- Card-based layout with soft shadows (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08))

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal subtle gold underlines
- Smooth transitions on card interactions (200ms ease-in-out)
- Micro-interactions on CTA buttons with slight scale and shadow depth

**Animation:**
- Fade-in on scroll for domain cards (staggered 100ms delays)
- Subtle slide-up entrance for testimonials
- Smooth color transitions on hover (150ms)

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) for headlines—conveys authority and premium positioning
- Body: Inter (sans-serif) for readability
- Hierarchy: 48px/56px for h1, 32px for h2, 18px for body text
- Font weights: 400 (body), 600 (emphasis), 700 (headlines)

## Selected Design: Modern Institutional

I've selected the **Modern Institutional** approach for this landing page. This design philosophy aligns perfectly with the product's target audience (M&A professionals, PE analysts, VC deal teams) and the core value proposition of precision, rigor, and professional authority.

**Why this choice:**
- Finance professionals expect clarity and institutional credibility
- The asymmetric layout and gold accents signal premium positioning without being ostentatious
- The restrained color palette and typography convey confidence and expertise
- The card-based domain layout makes the 60-point framework immediately scannable
- Micro-interactions and subtle animations add polish without distraction

**Key design decisions to enforce:**
- Navy + gold color scheme throughout
- Playfair Display for headlines (premium serif)
- Asymmetric layouts with left-aligned content
- Vertical gold accents on key sections
- Soft shadows and card-based components
- Staggered animations on scroll for domain cards
